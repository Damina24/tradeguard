# Account Deletion Page — TradeGuard

Required by Google Play and the App Store for any app with account creation.
Host this content at a public URL: `https://__PLACEHOLDER_DOMAIN__/delete-account`
Enter that URL in Play Console → App content → Data safety → Data deletion, and in
App Store Connect → App Privacy.

---

## Delete your TradeGuard account and data

You can delete your account and associated data at any time. Two ways:

### Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

1. Open TradeGuard and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm when prompted (a second confirmation is required).
5. Your deletion request is processed immediately.

### Option 2 — By email

Email **privacy@__PLACEHOLDER_DOMAIN__** from your registered address with the subject
**"Delete my TradeGuard data"**. We will verify and action the request.

## What is deleted

- Profile and login (email, user ID)
- Broker account connections and stored MetaAPI identifiers
- Trades, equity snapshots, signal follows and AI reviews
- Uploaded chart screenshots
- Push tokens and app preferences

Data is deleted or anonymised within **30 days**.

## What may be retained

- Purchase/subscription and tax records required for legal compliance are retained in
  anonymised form (no email in plain text) for the period required by law.
- Aggregated, non-identifying statistics may remain.

## Subscription note

Deleting your account does **not** automatically cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in
your **Google Play** or **Apple App Store** subscription settings to stop billing.

## Deactivate vs delete

Deactivation (sign out) only ends your session and keeps your data. Deletion permanently
removes your data as described above and cannot be undone.
