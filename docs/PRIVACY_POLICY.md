# Privacy Policy — TradeGuard

Effective date: __PLACEHOLDER_DATE__
App: TradeGuard - Forex Signals (`com.tradeguard.app`)
Controller: Strategy Tracker ("we", "us")
Contact: privacy@__PLACEHOLDER_DOMAIN__

## 1. Summary

TradeGuard is an analytics and education app for forex traders. We do not execute trades as
a broker and we never store your broker password. We collect the minimum data required to
run the guard, journal and signals features you use.

## 2. Data We Collect

| Category | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account | email, user ID | authentication |
| Financial (optional) | trade fills, P&L, lots, account balances | guard math, journal, analytics |
| Photos (optional) | chart screenshots you upload | AI review, trade notes |
| App activity | guard screen opens, signals followed | feature function, analytics |
| Diagnostics | crash logs | stability |
| Device identifiers | FCM push token, purchase token | notifications, subscriptions |

We do **not** collect location, contacts, SMS, or health data.

## 3. Broker Connection

Broker credentials you enter are transmitted to our processor MetaAPI to establish a
read-only session and are **not stored** by us. We store only the MetaAPI account
identifier. Live copy-trading requires separate, explicit consent.

## 4. How We Use Data

Provide and secure the service; compute drawdown/daily-loss guard values; generate AI trade
reviews and chart analysis; send push alerts; process subscriptions; prevent fraud; comply
with law.

## 5. Sharing

We share data only with service providers acting on our behalf: Supabase (hosting/database),
MetaAPI (broker relay), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Firebase (push), OpenAI (AI processing).
We do **not** sell your data and do **not** use it for advertising. Service providers may not
use your data for their own purposes.

## 6. AI Processing

Trade and chart data sent for AI analysis is processed to return a result and is not used to
train third-party models under our configured settings. Do not upload data you are not
authorised to share.

## 7. Retention

Trade data is retained while your account is active. Equity snapshots are retained up to 13
months. On account deletion we delete or anonymise data within 30 days, except records we
must keep for tax/legal compliance.

## 8. Security

TLS in transit, encryption at rest, row-level security, short-lived broker sessions, no
password storage. No system is perfectly secure; use a unique password.

## 9. Your Rights

Access, export, correct, and delete your data. Use Settings → Delete Account or email
privacy@__PLACEHOLDER_DOMAIN__. You may also object to or restrict certain processing where
law provides. EU/UK users may lodge a complaint with their supervisory authority.

## 10. Children

The app is for users 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 11. International Transfers

Data may be processed in countries outside your own. We rely on appropriate safeguards where
required.

## 12. Changes

We will update this policy and change the effective date. Material changes will be notified
in-app.

## 13. Not Financial Advice

TradeGuard is analytics and education only, is not a broker or advisor, and provides no
investment recommendation. Trading forex and CFDs carries a high risk of loss.
